## What you get

Players who pre-register for Vibe Racer on Google Play receive the "Pre-Registration Launch Pack", a free in-game bonus, when the game becomes available and they install it.

## Terms

1. The reward is free. Pre-registering costs nothing and no payment is needed to receive it.
2. Each Google account can receive the reward once.
3. The reward is delivered automatically inside the game after installation, once the game is released. You may need to open the game and be online to receive it.
4. The reward has no cash value, cannot be exchanged or transferred, and is valid only in Vibe Racer.
5. The reward content may change before release (for example, the number of coins or items), but it will always be a free in-game bonus.
6. If you remove your pre-registration before launch, you may not receive the reward.
7. The reward is available only in countries and regions where pre-registration is offered.
8. We may cancel or change the reward if required by law or by Google Play policies. Google Play's terms apply to pre-registration.
