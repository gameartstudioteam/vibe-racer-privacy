# Privacy Policy for Vibe Racer

Effective date: October 16, 2026  
Developer: GameArtForKids ("we", "us")  
Contact: thezzxc@gmail.com  
App: Vibe Racer (Android package `com.GameArtForKids.VibeRacer`)

This policy explains what information Vibe Racer ("the game") uses. The game has no account system and no server of our own. It does not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or precise location.

## 1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, cars, levels, settings, best scores, timers) is kept in a local save file on your device. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## 2. Information collected through third-party services

**Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics).** We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and to improve it. It collects an app instance identifier, device and app information (model, operating system version, app version, language), approximate location derived from your IP address, and events about how you play: screens opened, races started and finished, level progress, buttons used, purchases made (product, price, currency) and ads watched. The game also derives a coarse region (for example a country code) once from your device's SIM, network or language settings to choose a starter car, and reports it as an analytics property. We do not set a user id and do not link this data to your identity. Where the game asks for your consent (see Advertising below), analytics data is collected only if you allow analytics storage; if you do not, nothing is sent to Firebase Analytics, and if you allow it later in the game's Settings (PRIVACY), collection starts from then.

**Advertising (Google AdMob).** The game shows ads (banner, interstitial, rewarded video and app-open ads) served by Google AdMob only; no other ad network is used. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your Android advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland, some US states) the game shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and you can change that choice later in the game's Settings (PRIVACY). Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.

**Purchases (Google Play Billing, Unity IAP).** In-app purchases are processed by Google Play through Unity's in-app purchasing library. We get confirmation of a purchase, not your payment details.

**Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services).** If you sign in to Google Play Games, your player id and display name are used to submit and show leaderboard scores. This is handled by Google. You can decline and still play.

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders locally on your device (for example when a gift or fuel is ready). Nothing is sent to a server.

## 3. How we use the information

To run the game, show and measure ads, process purchases, analyse and improve the game, and prevent fraud and abuse.

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data goes to Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play, Google Play Games) and to Unity (in-app purchasing), as described in section 2. We share it with no one else unless the law requires it.

## 5. Your choices

- Advertising ID: reset or delete it, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
- Consent: use the PRIVACY option in the game's Settings where it is offered.
- Notifications: change them in Android system settings.
- Deletion: uninstall the game to delete the local save. To ask us to delete data we may hold, write to thezzxc@gmail.com. Data held by Google and Unity is managed through their own tools.

## 6. Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age set by the law of your country, for example 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, write to thezzxc@gmail.com.

## 7. Security and retention

The Google and Unity SDKs send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Retention of analytics and advertising data is set by Google. Firebase Analytics keeps event-level data for 2 months (the Google Analytics default); aggregated reports are kept by Google.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above changes when we do.
